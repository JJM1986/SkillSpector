## Personas
- Professional & Trustworthy: Clean, certificates, experience highlights. For technical trades.
- Friendly Local Hero: Warm colors, community photos, personal story. For services.
- Modern Expert: Bold, tech-forward, AI features prominent. For IT or innovative.
- Classic Craft: Textures, wood/metal accents, tradition. For handwerk.
- Energetic Service: Bright, call-to-actions strong. For high-volume.

Select 1 primary persona per site. Generate visual style accordingly.
